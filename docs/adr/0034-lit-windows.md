# 0034. Lit windows

Status: superseded by [ADR 0037](0037-quiet-room.md), 2026-11-01, except the overcast layer, the heat strip's data and the /stats layout, which stand. Accepted 2026-10-26. Amends ADR 0026 where it changes the room's visual system, and ADR 0029 (overcast). Amended 2026-10-31: quieter windows and stats (below); it replaces the window chrome, bar colours, status bar, sample tag, big numbers and heat caption described above.

## Context

The owner found the site flat and generic under its one illustrated scene: panels were solid fills with stepped corners, one typeface voice at every size, and `/stats` read as a table of facts. Three prototypes were built over the real pages. The owner first picked "Pixel Desk" (windows, a pixel typeface, scanlines), then saw it on a Preview and found the pixel face hard to read and mushy. The approved direction is a hybrid of "Lamplight" (a lit room, a soft serif display face) and Pixel Desk's windows. This ADR records the build; the prototypes were overlays on built HTML and none of their code is reused.

## Decision

### Type

Fraunces for the name, headings, post titles and big numbers. Source Serif 4 still sets everything read; JetBrains Mono the data, dates, slugs, window title bars, buttons and the footer. No pixel face anywhere, no text-shadow on text, no glow, no scanlines, no lowercased headings.

Fraunces is a variable font (OFL 1.1, no Reserved Font Name) with four axes. The build keeps none: the subset is one static cut, weight 750, the soft form (`SOFT` 100), the wonky glyphs (`WONK` 1) and an optical size of 48, which reads well from 1.2 rem sub-headings to a 3.8 rem title. Every display use is that weight. `just fonts` fetches the TTF, checks its sha256, subsets it to what a heading needs (ASCII, Latin-1 letters and the typographic punctuation, with kerning and ligatures but not the text faces' small caps and figure styles) and writes `display.woff2`: 14.3 KB. The first cut, a weight axis from 600 to 800 and the whole Latin-1 set, was 30.8 KB and put mobile home LCP at 1.96 s against ADR 0017's 2.0 s; this one measures 1.81 s. The latest tagged release (1.000, October 2020) predates the variable font, so the build pins upstream's latest commit (7ccdec3, 2025-10-21, font version 1.003); `@fontsource-variable/fraunces` 5.3.0 is the same family. `font-display: swap` as for the others (ADR 0026). It is preloaded only where a large title would otherwise rewrap when it swaps in (a post, a TIL note and /writing): a preload of it and the mono on every page moved mobile LCP on /projects, /about and /uses from about 1.4 s to 1.7 s in CI (ADR 0017 flags one simulated round trip, 150 ms, as the limit of noise). Everywhere else headings swap in through a fallback face: while the display face loads they are set in the serif roman (already downloaded, the same file) scaled to 101.4% of its width and given Fraunces' ascent and descent. `font-display: optional` was tried, as was taking the face out entirely: neither moved Lighthouse's mobile LCP reliably, because that number steps by one simulated round trip (150 ms) from run to run. Run against itself, the unchanged base build reads 1,208 ms in one pair of runs and 1,505 ms in the next, which the regression rule (more than 10 % and more than 200 ms) reports as a regression; the display face adds one 14 KB request, about 70 ms at the throttled 1.6 Mbit/s. A failed LCP check that names one page and shows a 150 or 300 ms step is therefore re-run, and the owner decides if it keeps failing. The /stats fixtures banner is set in the serif, which is preloaded, rather than the mono, whose swap rewrapped it (CLS 0.16 against the 0.01 budget). Home loads three fonts (`e2e/room.spec.ts` checks which). Measured again against main with three runs per page: the display face is requested before LCP on home and /about whichever way it is declared, and costs one simulated step (150 ms) there. Preloading it on every page, with or without `fetchpriority="low"`, moved no page; `font-display: optional` moved /about by one step in two runs of three and home not at all, and would drop the face on a first visit, so it was not kept. Taking the face out entirely still left /about one step above main, so the font is not the whole difference.

**Font budget (ADR 0017).** 127.4 of 200 KB for the whole build, up from 113 KB: the display face adds 14.3 KB. Home loads the serif roman, the mono and the display face, 87 KB.

### Palette and light

Lamplight's palette: night ground aubergine `#0d0a17`, amber lamp `#ffb54a`, mint `#6ee7b0`, rose `#ff8e7c`, cream text `#f6ead2`; day is a sunlit plaster wall `#efe3cb` with the lamp darkened to `#7a3a00`, the mint to `#0b4f3c` and the muted text to `#4a3f4d`. The page has a warm pool of light at the top right and a faint mint one at the left; a post sits under the lamp's pool, centred. Colour comes from lit surfaces, not glow.

### Windows

`.panel` and `.paper` are windows, as in the Pixel Desk build: a 3 px border in the window's colour, a crisp title bar with a file name in mono (`projects.json`, `listening.app`, `shipping.log`, `llm-usage.db`, `shelf.md`, `writing.md`; a page may set its own with `--title`, or `data-file` as `/uses` does), three small square buttons, and a hard 7 px offset shadow with no blur. The file name and the buttons are CSS content, so assistive technology does not read them; the window's own heading is the label. Inside, the panel is lit from its top left corner and falls off to its bottom right in its object's colour (`--screen`/`-hi`/`-lo`, and so on: green for projects, violet for listening, amber for shipping, blue for LLM usage, brown for the shelf, a sage for about, aubergine for writing). The popover Menu and the dialogs share the chrome. The stepped `clip-path` corners are gone.

**Contrast.** Title-bar text is cream (`--bar-text`) on bars chosen for it: night violet `#5a4fd6` 4.97:1, green `#176b4e` 5.39:1, amber `#8a5410` 5.22:1, blue `#1f6a96` 4.92:1, brown `#8a5a3a` 4.86:1, red `#a83a30` 5.29:1; by day every bar is at least 6.4:1. The footer is a status bar in the chrome colour with the same text. Text on a lit panel was computed at all three stops of its gradient (axe cannot read a gradient), in both themes: body text at least 8.3:1 at night and 10:1 by day, muted text at least 4.8:1 and 6.0:1, links and mint at least 5.7:1 and 5.2:1. The day gradients are kept shallow for this reason.

### Shipping, alive

The list is a git graph: a dashed rail, a square node per event, the newest amber and blinking, each time in mint. Above it a heat strip of the last 28 UTC days, one cell a day in the lamp's amber, drawn from the events already in the snapshot (`heatLevels` in `src/data/format.ts`: levels 0 to 4 by events a day, computed at build against the build's time, so it adds no request and no script). It is one `role="img"` with a text label; its caption is real text, the first and last day with "as built" on the last (`3 Oct`, `30 Oct, as built`), and the label says the same. On /stats the strip sits above the list and is drawn once with the page; the script that keeps the list current (ADR 0015) redraws only the list. It is not redrawn from a live answer because the answer holds at most 50 events, which need not reach back 28 days, so a redrawn strip would show quiet days that are only missing data; the dated caption keeps it true as the list refreshes. The cells and the box are opaque, and the four levels are steps of lightness, each at least 3:1 against the box and 1.3:1 from the level below (`e2e/contrast.spec.ts`). Times are the relative ones the live pages already keep (ADR 0015), in mint.

### Sample data

In fixtures mode (ADR 0015) every panel's "Sample data, not live" line is a solid rose tag in mono capitals, and /stats opens with a hatched rose banner inside a rose border. Rose is the colour of what is not real; the text on both is at least 5.2:1.

### Posts and other pages

Every post and TIL note starts with a server-rendered file bar: `~/writing/<slug>.md` and `<n> min read`, from the Markdown's word count at 220 a minute (`readingMinutes`). Search leaves it out. The 404 is a "file not found" dialog (an `error.txt` window) with a link home. The /skills shelf cards are small windows. Big numbers on `/stats` are Fraunces in the lamp's colour.

### Motion

`steps()` only: the newest shipping node and the status-bar dot blink, the record turns in 36 steps and the lamp comes on in 9. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the existing global rule stops all of it. No sound. The proposed delights that need script or more markup (command-line search, the Konami rain, collapsing windows) are not built.

### Overcast

A code-3 sky (ADR 0029) has its own layer, `weather-overcast-{day,night}.png`, drawn by `scripts/scene.py` in the room's 41 colours (1.4 and 1.8 KB). Code 2 keeps the puffs. The brief also named cloud cover of 100 as a trigger; it is not one, because the room script has 1 B of headroom under its 2,300 B cap (ADR 0017) and a code 2 answer with a full cover reading is rare.

## Consequences

- Fonts +14 KB (127 of 200 KB); the source CSS about +1.6 KB gzip; home JavaScript unchanged apart from 4 B in the room clock for the new weather state, 2,299 of 2,300 B.
- Home loads one more font than before.
- Every window needs a file name. Since the 2026-10-31 amendment a new material is a `--dot` colour and, if its links differ, an `--accent` checked at the three gradient stops in both themes.
- Fraunces is for display only; running text stays in Source Serif 4.
- Mobile LCP costs more than the font bytes suggest. The display face adds about one to two simulated steps (150 to 400 ms) of mobile LCP in CI against main: main's base measured about 1,386 ms (bc7b54b), and this branch reads 1,670 to 1,820 ms on a post, /writing and /stats. Every page stays under ADR 0017's 2.0 s budget, but the regression rule (more than 10 % and more than 200 ms) fails on a post. The owner accepted the cost on 2026-10-30 and #39 merged past that check once. Main's next push re-seeds the base, so later PRs compare against this design.

## Amendment, 2026-10-31: quiet windows

The owner found the site read as sloppy rather than as a polished, minimal developer site. Every window had its own saturated title bar and a hard 7 px shadow, so a page of six windows was six competing colours; /stats repeated 3.2 rem numbers in the lamp's colour, wrapped typing bests over two lines, and showed an empty Shelf box. The room, the palette, the fonts and the window idea stay. What changed:

- **One window treatment.** Every `.panel` and `.paper` is the same lit surface (`--surface-hi` to `--surface-lo`, the gradient the paper had), with a 1 px `--rule` edge and no shadow. The title bar is a faint tint with a hairline under it and the file name in `--fg-2`, mono 0.75 rem. The window's colour is one 0.5 rem square before the file name (`--dot`: mint for projects and about, violet for listening, amber for shipping and the default, cyan for LLM usage, brown for the shelf, rose for writing and the 404) and its links (`--accent`). The three buttons are gone. The per-material tokens (`--screen`, `--vinyl`, `--cork` and the rest), `--shade`, `--bar-text` and `--rose-bar` are gone with them. The /skills cards, the Menu popover and the dialogs use the same edge.
- **Header and footer.** The header ends in a 1 px rule, and the current page is underlined in the lamp's colour instead of filled. The footer is a status line, not a bar: a rule, then muted text and links on the ground, the lamp on hover; its focus ring is the lamp on the ground (`e2e/contrast.spec.ts`).
- **Section links.** "All writing", "More" and "All projects" are quiet mono links with an arrow, not outlined capitals.
- **Sample data.** The tag is a rose outline with rose text in mono, not a solid capitals block; the /stats banner's border is 1 px. The day rose is darkened to `#952f24` so the tag text stays above 4.5:1 on the darkest stop of a window (5.06:1).
- **Stats.** /stats is two stacks of windows from 60 rem (listening, projects, typing; shipping, LLM usage), one column on a phone. A stat is a small mono label over a 1.6 rem value that never wraps; typing bests are aligned rows of label, speed, accuracy and day. Long lists show five rows and fold the rest into a disclosure that opens without JavaScript (recent tracks, shipping events, star counts), which takes mobile /stats from about 5,450 px to about 4,250 px at 360 px. The projects window lists the five featured projects with the most stars. The Shelf window is not shown until the site reads `/v1/shelf`, and the shelf drops out of the room's links with it. The shipping strip's caption reads "One square a day, 4 Oct to 31 Oct". The projects window owns the featured repositories' star counts (the projects source, every 6 hours); shipping's list, "Stars on other repositories", keeps only the others (repo-pulse's daily count, each row "22 stars on 23 October"), so no repository shows two counts on one page. Shipping, projects and typing are drawn from one markup module for the build and the browser (`src/lib/stats-panels.ts`).
- **Home and about.** The room's list never leaves one object alone on a row: two columns on a phone (the odd one spans the row), four on a tablet, one row from 60 rem. The name lost its full stop. Before the first published post, the Writing window lists the latest TIL notes. /about sets the note on the name beside the summary from 52 rem, and the resume in two columns from 60 rem.

**Contrast.** Computed on the three stops of the window gradient in both themes: body text at least 10.9:1 by day and 12.8:1 at night, muted text (file names included) 6.5:1 and 7.3:1, the lamp 5.6:1 and 8.7:1, mint 6.2:1 and 9.9:1, rose 5.1:1 and 6.8:1. `--line`, the edge of inputs, buttons, `kbd`, the Menu and the dialogs, is `#7f6b57` by day and `#716aa8` at night: at least 3.5:1 against the ground and the surface in both themes, so a text field's border meets WCAG 1.4.11 (`e2e/contrast.spec.ts` checks the search input). The Menu and the dialogs also cast a soft shadow, since they sit over the page.
